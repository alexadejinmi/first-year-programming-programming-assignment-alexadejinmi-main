Build: Cars → Frogger (exercises/cars-frogger/) — no further Car stage: cars never bounce, so that track's last stage was stage 12 (screen-wrap, mirroring sketch 12). This is where the capstone begins instead, continuing directly from your stage-12 Car sketch.

Capstone — Build Frogger. Build the following in sequence; each stage starts from your own solution to the previous one.

  • Stage 1 — add a player. Add a player character (a circle or small square, called the "frog") starting at the bottom-centre of the canvas. Give it a position and move it with the arrow keys — either keyPressed() with the LEFT_ARROW/RIGHT_ARROW/UP_ARROW/DOWN_ARROW constants, or keyIsDown() checked every frame in draw() for continuous movement (look up both in the p5 reference and pick one). Clamp the player's position so it can't leave the canvas, reusing this sketch's clamping idea.
  • Stage 2 — lay out the lanes. Arrange the cars array into several horizontal lanes (a handful of shared y values), giving each lane's cars a consistent speed and direction — some moving left, others right. Mostly a matter of choosing good numbers in setup().
  • Stage 3 — collision. Detect when the player overlaps a car. Balls used dist() because they're circles; the player and cars here are rectangles, so use a rectangle-overlap ("AABB") test instead — two rectangles overlap only if they overlap on both the x-axis and the y-axis at the same time. On a collision, reset the player to the start position.
  • Stage 4 — winning. Add a "safe zone" at the top of the canvas. When the player reaches it, display a message with text() (e.g. "You win!") and stop responding to further key presses until the game is reset.
